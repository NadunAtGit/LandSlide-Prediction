1. Landslides.zip

This folder contains landslide boundary demarcation data in the following formats:

- ESRI Shapefile format
  (Can be opened in ArcMap, ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, or any other GIS software)

- Google Earth Pro KML format
  (Can be opened in Google Earth Pro)

Software Download Links:
QGIS (Free & Open Source):
https://qgis.org/en/site/forusers/download.html

Google Earth Pro:
https://www.google.com/earth/versions/#earth-pro

----Data Used-----------------------------------------------

Pre_Disaster.zip

Contains a mosaic of Sentinel-2 RGB composite images
(Modified Copernicus Sentinel data)
© European Union (Copernicus Programme)

Acquisition period:
20 July 2025 to 20 November 2025


Post_Disaster.zip

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data
© European Union (Copernicus Programme)

Acquisition dates:
30 November 2025
02 December 2025
07 December 2025

The data is used under the Copernicus Open Access Licence.

Note:
Clouds present in the satellite images have been masked and may appear
in black or white colours at different zoom levels.

Geographic Coordinate System:

------------------------------------------------------------

Important Note:

For accurate area calculations or distance measurements, users are advised
to reproject the data to one of the following projected coordinate systems:

- SLD99
- WGS84 / UTM Zone 44N
